Climate Change

A global environmental issue affecting Earth’s climate system

Overview

Climate change refers to long-term changes in temperature and weather patterns. Human activities like burning fossil fuels are the main cause of recent changes.

Causes

  • Burning fossil fuels (coal, oil, gas)
  • Deforestation
  • Industrial emissions
  • Transport pollution

Effects

  • Rising sea levels
  • Extreme weather events
  • Wildfires
  • Loss of wildlife habitats

Solutions

  • Use renewable energy
  • Reduce plastic waste
  • Plant more trees
  • Use public transport

Scientific Evidence

Scientists use satellites, ice cores, and weather data to track climate change.

  • Global temperatures are rising
  • Ice sheets are melting
  • Sea levels are increasing
