About Climate Change

Climate change refers to long-term shifts in temperatures and weather patterns. Human activities, especially burning fossil fuels, are the main cause.

Effects

  • Rising sea levels
  • More extreme weather events
  • Loss of wildlife habitats
  • Increased global temperatures

Solutions

  • Use renewable energy (solar, wind)
  • Reduce plastic waste
  • Plant more trees
  • Save electricity and water
