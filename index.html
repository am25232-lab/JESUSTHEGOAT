Climate Change

A global environmental challenge affecting Earth’s systems

Overview

Climate change refers to long-term shifts in temperature, weather patterns, and climate systems on Earth. While climate has changed naturally over time, current changes are happening much faster due to human activities.

Causes

  • Burning fossil fuels – releases carbon dioxide (CO₂)
  • Deforestation – reduces Earth’s ability to absorb CO₂
  • Industrial pollution – increases greenhouse gases
  • Transport emissions – cars, planes, and ships add pollution

Effects

  • Rising sea levels and coastal flooding
  • More extreme weather (storms, heatwaves, floods)
  • Wildfires becoming more frequent
  • Loss of biodiversity and animal habitats

Solutions

  • Switch to renewable energy (solar, wind)
  • Reduce plastic and waste
  • Plant trees and protect forests
  • Use public transport or electric vehicles

Scientific Evidence

Scientists have confirmed climate change through decades of data collected from satellites, ice cores, and weather stations.

  • Global temperatures have risen significantly since the 1800s
  • Ice sheets in Greenland and Antarctica are shrinking
  • Sea levels are rising worldwide
